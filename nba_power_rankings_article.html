Power Rankings: Undefeated Grizzlies surge into Top 5

Memphis, Dallas and Golden State make major leaps in the Power Rankings before Week 3 of the 2026-27 season.

John Schuhmann

Archive
November 2, 2026 9:19 PM

Rookie forward Cameron Boozer and the Grizzlies are 6-0 before Week 3 of the regular season.

The last undefeated team in the league is … the Memphis Grizzlies?

The Grizzlies finished 12 games out of the Play-In Tournament last season. They’re led by a guard who’s averaged 9.7 points per game over his career and a forward who made his NBA debut just 12 days ago.

Now they’re the 21st team in the 22 years since the league expanded to 30 teams to begin the season with six straight wins.

Of those 20 other teams that were 6-0:

  • All 20 went on to make the playoffs.
  • 19 of the 20 finished as a No. 5 seed or higher, with 10 earning the No. 1 seed in their conference.
  • 18 of the 20 won a playoff series, 12 reached the conference finals, seven reached the Finals, and four won the championship.

The two teams that started the season 6-0 and didn’t win a playoff series were the 2010-11 New Orleans Hornets (No. 7 seed) and the 2022-23 Bucks, who were the No. 1 seed but lost to the No. 8 seed Miami Heat in five games. Giannis Antetokounmpo missed Games 2 and 3 after leaving Game 1 with a back injury.

All in all, those are good numbers for the Grizzlies. But how much longer can they keep this streak going? Their November schedule begins with home games against the Pelicans and Pacers before they host the Thunder in Emirates NBA Cup action on Friday.

Plus-Minus Players of the Week

  • Right Way: Jabari Smith Jr. (HOU) was a plus-59 in four games last week.
  • Wrong Way: Zach LaVine (SAC) and Leonard Miller (CHI) were both  minus-68 in three games last week.

Teams of the Week

  • Make It Last Forever: Golden State (4-0) — The Rockets and Warriors are the only teams with three wins over teams currently over .500, and a;; three of the Warriors’ wins came last week.
  • Something Just Ain’t Right: Indiana (0-4) — Tyrese Haliburton is great, but the Pacers shouldn’t be losing to both the Bucks and the Nets (teams that are a combined 0-10 otherwise), whether he’s in the lineup or not.

East vs. West

  • The West is 10-2 (.833) against the East in interconference games after going 5-1 last week.

Movement in the Rankings

  • High jumps of the week: Memphis (+12), Golden State (+11)
  • Free falls of the week: Indiana (-10), Minnesota (-9)

Week 3 Team to Watch

  • Cleveland — The Cavs are off to a solid but unconvincing 4-2 start. They could use some signature wins, and this week’s schedule — four games against the Thunder (x 2), Sixers and Knicks — gives them an opportunity to prove they’re a serious contender.

Previous Power Rankings

  • Last week: Pistons, Timberwolves climb into Top 3
  • The archive: NBA.com Power Rankings

Key Stats to Know

OffRtg: Points scored per 100 possessions (League Rank)
DefRtg: Points allowed per 100 possessions (League Rank)
NetRtg: Point differential per 100 possessions (League Rank)

The league has averaged 111.9 points scored per 100 possessions and 101.2 possessions (per team) per 48 minutes this season.

A rest-advantage game (or rest-disadvantage game) is one where one team played the day/night before, but the other (the team with the advantage) did not. Teams are 8-8 in rest-advantage games, 6-3 at home and 2-5 on the road this season.

* = Emirates NBA Cup game

NBA.com’s Power Rankings, released every Monday during the season, are just one man’s opinion. If you have an issue with the rankings, or have a question or comment for John Schuhmann, contact him via Bluesky.

#1
Houston Rockets
Last Week:4↑

Record: 6-1

OffRtg: 121.2 (1) DefRtg: 108.9 (9) NetRtg: +12.3 (2)

The Rockets lost for the first time when they played without Alperen Sengun and missed some critical free throws in Dallas on Friday. But Sengun was back in the lineup the following night, when they beat the Thunder by 21 points.

Two takeaways

  • According to tracking data, the Rockets have seen the league’s biggest improvement in shot quality, jumping from 22nd last season to seventh through the first two weeks. Kevin Durant’s shot quality is his highest mark in the 14 seasons for which we have tracking data, and he’s also registering a career-high free throw rate of 63 attempts per 100 shots from the field. But he went 0-for-2 from the line with the Rockets down two and less than two minutes left in Dallas on Friday.
  • The Rockets held the Bucks to fewer than one point per possession without Sengun last week, but the defense has still been at its best (99.4 allowed per 100) with him on the floor. Some of that is opponent 3-point shooting (25%), but it’s also about free throws and rebounding.

Coming up: The road team has won the last five meetings between the Rockets and Celtics, with both of last season’s games decided by more than 20 points. Their meeting in Houston on Monday will be the Rockets’ fourth game against an Eastern Conference opponent.

Week 3: vs. BOS, vs. MIN, @ PHX

#2
Detroit Pistons
Last Week:2

Record: 5-1

OffRtg: 111.3 (17) DefRtg: 103.2 (1) NetRtg: +8.1 (6)

The Pistons were outscored by 38 points (63-25) in the second and third quarters in New York on Tuesday, losing to the Knicks for the first time in almost 18 months. But they took care of business against the Hornets and Nets and are off to their best six-game start (5-1) in the last 11 years.

Two takeaways

  • The Pistons held their opponent to fewer than one point per possession just twice through Dec. 31 last season. They’ve already done so three times this year, even with Isaiah Stewart in Memphis and Jalen Duren having played just 38 total minutes. They’ve allowed just 91.3 per 100 in 78 minutes with Ausar Thompson and Paul Reed on the floor together.
  • But their current starting lineup (with Reed in place of Duren) has struggled offensively, scoring just 62 points on 79 possessions (78.5 per 100). After some terrific first quarters on their three-game road trip, the Pistons trailed by double digits in the first half against both Charlotte and Brooklyn last week.

Coming up: The Pistons have two games left on a stretch where they’re playing six straight against teams that rank in the bottom 11 offensively. They’ve played a role in where those teams rank, of course, with their Week 1 win in Philadelphia being the Sixers’ worst offensive game of the season. They were without Joel Embiid, who could be available in Detroit on Wednesday.

Week 3: @ CHA, vs. PHI, vs. LAL

#3
Philadelphia 76ers
Last Week:5↑

Record: 6-1

OffRtg: 108.6 (23) DefRtg: 104.9 (3) NetRtg: +3.7 (11)

The Sixers are 6-1, even though they have just the league’s 11th-best point differential (plus-3.9 per game). All six wins were within five points in the last five minutes, while the one loss (a rest-disadvantage game without Joel Embiid against Detroit) was not.

Two takeaways

  • VJ Edgecombe is just 8-for-27 (29.6%) on non-clutch 3-pointers, but he’s 2-for-4 in the clutch, having given the Sixers 4-point leads in almost identical scenarios against New York (a catch-and-shoot 3 with 32.6 seconds left) and Charlotte (a step-back 3 with 35.4 seconds left). Tyrese Maxey is tied for the league lead with 21 total points scored in the clutch, and the Sixers have scored 59 points on their 45 clutch offensive possessions (131 per 100).
  • Maxey is averaging nearly 38 minutes per game (second in the league), and the offense has struggled (94.4 points scored per 100) with him off the floor. But Anfernee Simons has averaged 20 points off the bench over the last three games, and he was on the floor down the stretch in both games of their weekend back-to-back.

Coming up: The Sixers won’t have another back-to-back until Nov. 15-16, and they’ll have a pair of marquee rematches when they visit the Pistons and Cavs on the three-game trip that begins Wednesday in Detroit.

Week 3: vs. CHI, @ DET, @ CLE*, @ CHI

#4
San Antonio Spurs
Last Week:6↑

Record: 4-2

OffRtg: 115.2 (9) DefRtg: 108.5 (8) NetRtg: +6.7 (8)

The Spurs lost their first two games of the season, but have since won four straight. They’ve scored 119.2 points per 100 possessions over the streak, with Victor Wembanyama totaling 79 points over the last two games.

Two takeaways

  • Not surprisingly, Stephon Castle has had the biggest offensive adjustment with Dylan Harper moving into the starting lineup this season. Castle has gone from a team-high 5.7 minutes per game of possession (19% of his minutes on the floor) last season to 3.9 per game (third on the team and 13% of his minutes) this season. His efficiency is down and he had his first scoreless game in almost two years on Saturday.
  • But Castle’s overall scoring and usage rate isn’t down significantly. He’s just playing more off the ball and averaging 6.3 catch-and-shoot 3-point attempts per game, up from just 2.9 per game last season. The Spurs are in the top six in both ball and player movement and lead the league by a wide margin in the percentage of their 3-point attempts (84%) that have been off the catch.

Coming up: The winning streak includes wins over the Jazz, Mavs and Kings, who the Spurs will face again in the next five days. The Mavs were without both Kyrie Irving and Cooper Flagg when the Spurs won in Dallas in Week 1.

Week 3: vs. UTA, vs. DAL, @ SAC*, @ POR

#5
Memphis Grizzlies
Last Week:17↑

Record: 6-0

OffRtg: 118.8 (3) DefRtg: 104.1 (2) NetRtg: +14.7 (1)

Cam Boozer recorded his first career triple-double as the Grizzlies won a battle of unbeaten teams in Portland on Thursday. Then they clobbered the Kings (without Ty Jerome) to make this the second 6-0 start in franchise history. The first came 12 years ago, when the Grizzlies finished 55-27 and lost in the conference semifinals after taking a 2-1 series lead over the eventual champion Warriors.

Two takeaways

  • The win in Portland was the third time that the Grizzlies shot below 50% in the paint. But they kept the Blazers in check on the other end and have been winning from the outside. They’ve outscored their opponent from 3-point range in all six of their games and by 15 points or more in each of the last four. The percentages are sure to start going the other way at some point, though. While the Grizzlies have shot a league-best 41.6% from beyond the arc, their opponents have shot just 30.1% (third lowest).
  • Jerome’s scoring has dipped below a point per minute, but Isaiah Stewart totaled 42 points in less than 39 minutes off the bench over the Grizzlies’ last two games. Bench minutes were much improved last week (plus-55 in Boozer’s 57 minutes off the floor) after the reserves struggled in Week 1.

Coming up: With the Pelicans and Pacers in town early this week, the Grizzlies have a good chance of keeping this streak going. Jerome isn’t on the injury report for the game against New Orleans on Monday.

Week 3: vs. NOP, vs. IND, vs. OKC*

#6
Boston Celtics
Last Week:8↑

Record: 5-1

OffRtg: 117.7 (5) DefRtg: 107.6 (6) NetRtg: +10.1 (4)

The Celtics have won five straight games after blowing a 22-point lead in the season opener. They’ve mostly been taking care of business against bad teams, though they did need a rookie mistake from Mikel Brown Jr. and a Jayson Tatum buzzer-beater to edge the Nets in overtime on Tuesday.

Two takeaways

  • The Celtics have been getting to the line. After averaging just 18.5 free throw attempts through their first two games, they’ve averaged 33.3 over the last four. Jayson Tatum has led the way, with 22 attempts in the overtime win over Brooklyn and another 17 in Orlando on Sunday afternoon. As things stand, only the Bulls have seen a bigger jump in free throw rate from last season (when the Celtics ranked 30th by a healthy margin).
  • With more free throws and the league’s best paint defense, the Celtics are a little less dependent on 3-point shooting. While the rest of the league is 21-62 (.253) when it’s been outscored from beyond the arc, the Celtics are 4-0.

Coming up: The Celtics have four games left on a stretch where they’re playing eight of nine against teams that currently have losing records. The lone exception is their visit to Houston on Monday. It’s one of two matchups this week between the six teams with fewer than two losses.

Week 3: @ HOU, vs. MIL, @ CHA*, @ MIL

#7
Oklahoma City Thunder
Last Week:1↓

Record: 4-2

OffRtg: 108.7 (21) DefRtg: 109.4 (11) NetRtg: -0.7 (17)

Chet Holmgren (right knee injury) has joined Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace on the list of Thunder players who are out long-term, and Alex Caruso has missed the last two games with a sprained ankle. After a 4-0 start, Oklahoma City has dropped two by a total of 45 points. That’s already as many losses by more than 20 as they had all of last season (two) and more than they had in 2024-25 (one).

Two takeaways

  • The three games that they’ve been without Williams, Wallace and Holmgren have been one of the Thunder’s worst defensive stretches (122.1 points allowed per 100 possessions) over the last three years. They’ve yielded 22 corner 3s over the three games (the league average is 3.6 per game) and forced just 11.8 turnovers per 100 possessions, down from 20.8 per 100 through their first three games.
  • Shai Gilgeous-Alexander’s true shooting percentage of 55.2% would be the lowest mark of his career, and it’s not just about struggles (30%) from beyond the arc. He’s also registering career-low marks for field goal percentage in the paint (50%) and free throw rate (21.7 attempts per 100 shots from the field). The good news is that the offense hasn’t been totally anemic with him off the floor over these last three games.

Coming up: The Thunder’s longest losing streak over the last three-plus seasons (since the start of 2023-24) was three games in April of 2024. To avoid matching that, they’ll need to win in Cleveland on Monday. That’s the second game of a stretch where they’re playing six of seven against teams that currently rank in the top eight offensively.

Week 3: @ CLE, vs. CLE, @ MEM*, vs. WAS

#8
Cleveland Cavaliers
Last Week:11↑

Record: 4-2

OffRtg: 115.8 (8) DefRtg: 111.5 (17) NetRtg: +4.3 (10)

The Cavs are the only team with multiple losses in games they led by at least 15 points, having blown a 15-point lead in Atlanta on Thursday. But they’ve won four of their last five, having handed the Wolves their first defeat (in a game the Cavs trailed by 15) earlier in the week.

Two takeaways

  • Each of the Cavs’ first five games was within five points in the final five minutes, and Sam Merrill has played twice as many clutch minutes as Jarrett Allen thus far. The Cavs have struggled (minus-15.0 points per 100 possessions) in 81 minutes with both Allen and Evan Mobley on the floor, but their one-big lineups (202 total minutes) have generally been terrific (plus-14.4 per 100), and the difference has been about even on both ends of the floor.
  • Some of the role players haven’t shot well from 3-point range, but Mobley is 17-for-27 (58%) from beyond the arc, with those 27 attempts accounting for 35% of his shots, the highest rate of his career by a healthy margin. He’s not going to keep shooting 58%, but he does look more comfortable with that shot than he did six months ago.

Coming up: The Cavs’ first blown lead came in their season opener, when they lost in Philadelphia after leading by as many as 29 points. They’ll meet the Sixers again on Friday, part of an intriguing week where they’ll play both of their games against the Thunder and face the Knicks for the first time.

Week 3: vs. OKC, @ OKC, vs. PHI*, vs. NYK

#9
Portland Trail Blazers
Last Week:15↑

Record: 4-1

OffRtg: 118.0 (4) DefRtg: 107.9 (7) NetRtg: +10.1 (5)

The Blazers went 1-1 in two games between unbeaten teams, outlasting the Lakers (on the road) in a barn-burner on Tuesday, but succumbing to the Grizzlies (at home) two nights later. This is their first 4-1 start since 2022-23, the last time they had Damian Lillard in uniform.

Two takeaways

  • Ja Morant is going to be out at least a month after suffering a hamstring injury on Thursday, and Scoot Henderson was in the starting lineup when the Blazers won in Utah over the weekend. They were good (plus-5.2 per 100 possessions) in 69 minutes with Morant and Lillard on the floor together, but have been terrific (plus-25.2 per 100) in 110 minutes with one on the floor without the other.
  • The Blazers and Lakers combined to score more than 130 points per 100 possessions in Portland’s win on Tuesday. L.A. has led the league in free throw differential in each of the last four seasons, but the Blazers had the edge last week and attempted 40 more free throws than their opponents over their last three games. Deni Avdija is still getting to the line a ton and Lillard’s free throw rate (43 attempts per 100 shots from the field) would be the second-highest of his career.

Coming up: The Blazers’ schedule remains tough for the next few weeks. They’ve lost their last 12 (and 22 of their last 24) games in Denver, where they’ll play on Monday. Their first back-to-back of the season – another visit to the Lakers and their first meeting with the Spurs – is over the weekend.

Week 3: @ DEN, @ PHX, @ LAL*, vs. SAS

#10
New York Knicks
Last Week:10

Record: 2-4

OffRtg: 109.6 (20) DefRtg: 110.6 (14) NetRtg: -1.1 (18)

The Knicks got their first win with a 40-point turnaround (from down 13 to up 27) against the team (Detroit) that swept them in convincing fashion last season. Another comeback win (in Chicago) followed, but they’re now 0-2 against the Sixers after an ugly offensive performance in Philadelphia on Friday.

Two takeaways

  • The Knicks have trailed by at least nine points in all six of their games, falling into double-digit holes in each of the last five. No team is more comfortable playing from behind, and the win over the Pistons was certainly a flashback to their postseason run. But double-digit deficits still mean something and, as a whole, the league is 30-77 (.280) when trailing by at least 10 points this season.
  • The Knicks rank 29th in initial offense, with only the Hornets having scored fewer points per 100 possessions before a rebound. But offensive boards have kept them somewhat afloat, with their 21.5 second-chance points per 100 leading the league. Andre Drummond (16.8%) ranks fourth in offensive rebounding percentage and Karl-Anthony Towns (10.3%) is registering the highest mark of his career.

Coming up: The Knicks have a pretty good chance of climbing to .500 when they host the Nets and Bulls early this week. They’ll have a rest advantage against Brooklyn and again when they play in Cleveland (Saturday) for their first meeting with the team they swept in the Eastern Conference finals.

Week 3: vs. BKN, vs. CHI, @ CLE

#11
Los Angeles Lakers
Last Week:7↓

Record: 5-2

OffRtg: 119.9 (2) DefRtg: 113.9 (20) NetRtg: +6.1 (9)

The Lakers have split the first four games of a stretch of five in seven days. They beat the Clippers without Luka Dončić on Wednesday, but then blew a 25-point lead (with him back in the lineup) at Golden State two nights later.

Two takeaways

  • The Lakers rank second offensively, even though they’re 28th in turnover rate, having committed more than 20 turnovers in each of their last three games. Dončić’s assist/turnover ratio of 1.33 would be the lowest of his career by a wide margin.
  • But the Lakers’ effective field goal percentage of 60.0% would be the highest mark in NBA history, and their free throw rate of 37.1 attempts per 100 shots from the field would be the second highest in the last 13 seasons. Dončić isn’t completing all his passes, but when he does, those passes have created the highest-quality shots in the league, according to tracking data.

Coming up: The Lakers will be at a rest disadvantage when they face the Heat on Monday, with Dončić having scored 60 points the last time the two teams met (in March). They’ll visit the Pistons over the weekend, against whom Dončić has averaged 35.1 points, the highest career average for any active player (in at least 10 games) against any opponent.

Week 3: vs. MIA, @ SAC, vs. POR*, @ DET

#12
Minnesota Timberwolves
Last Week:3↓

Record: 4-3

OffRtg: 116.0 (7) DefRtg: 105.0 (4) NetRtg: +11.1 (3)

Anthony Edwards scored 47 points in Cleveland on Monday … and was shut down (for multiple months) with a knee issue less than 24 hours later. The Wolves had their best offensive game of the season in a win over the Clippers on Friday night, but are 1-2 without their franchise player.

Two takeaways

  • With Edwards out and LaMelo Ball averaging just 25.2 minutes per game, the Wolves are now relying heavily on Ayo Dosunmu and Bones Hyland, with Jaden McDaniels also handling the ball more. Hyland had a nice offensive game (19 points, eight assists and just one turnover) off the bench in a win over the Clippers on Friday, but the Wolves have allowed 122.5 points per 100 possessions in his 82 minutes on the floor.
  • The Wolves had the league’s No. 1 defense through Friday, but they were also without Rudy Gobert when they lost a rest-disadvantage game in San Antonio on Saturday night. Their defensive success has been more about the perimeter than the interior, but while their opponents had shot just 23.6% from 3-point range through their first three games, they’ve shot 37.2% over the four games since.

Coming up: Done with their first set of five games in seven days, the Wolves won’t have another back-to-back until Nov. 16-17. They’ve got some teams they can beat without Edwards on the schedule this week, with their game against the Jazz on Sunday being the start of an eight-game homestand.

Week 3: vs. MIL, @ HOU, @ NOP*, vs. UTA

#13
Denver Nuggets
Last Week:12↓

Record: 3-2

OffRtg: 112.4 (14) DefRtg: 114.1 (21) NetRtg: -1.6 (19)

The Nuggets were the first team to lose to the 0-3 (at the time) Warriors, allowing Golden State to have the most efficient offensive game (134 points on 96 possessions) for any team this season. But Denver got back on the right side of .500 with wins in New Orleans and Phoenix.

Two takeaways

  • Last season (true shooting percentage of 62.2%) was the most efficient season of Jamal Murray’s career, and he’s been even sharper through five games this year (64.4%). Amazingly, that’s with him having made just four (24%) of his 17 mid-range attempts. His efficiency has been all about his 3-point shooting (23-for-43, 53%) and his career-high free throw rate (37.2 attempts per 100 shots from the field). He was 7-for-9 from deep and 12-for-15 from the line as he scored 39 points in Phoenix on Friday.
  • Only the Kings have allowed their opponents to take higher-quality shots than the Nuggets. Denver has the league’s highest rate (48%) of opponent shots that have come in the restricted area or from the corners, and it’s probably a little lucky that the Pelicans shot 3-for-24 on corner 3s over their two meetings.

Coming up: Before heading to Mexico City, the Nuggets will have two serious challenges at home, hosting the Blazers and Raptors. Nikola Jokić shot well, but didn’t get to the line much against Donovan Clingan last season, and they’ll meet again on Monday.

Week 3: vs. POR, vs. TOR, vs. IND (Mexico City)

#14
Dallas Mavericks
Last Week:20↑

Record: 4-2

OffRtg: 114.1 (12) DefRtg: 111.3 (15) NetRtg: +2.9 (12)

After a 1-2 start, the Mavs have won three straight games, handing both the Thunder and the Rockets their first losses of the season. Cooper Flagg missed just one game with his shoulder injury and has been the star of the streak, averaging 29.3 points, 6.7 rebounds, 8.3 assists, 2.3 steals and 1.3 blocks over the three games.

Two takeaways

  • Flagg had the ball in his hands down the stretch against Houston on Friday, and he put the game away with an iso floater (plus a foul) against Jabari Smith Jr. and an iso turnaround over Tari Eason. He’s shot 8-for-9 on clutch shots over the Mavs’ two games (both wins) that were within five points in the last five minutes, and he’s 11-for-14 from mid-range over the three-game winning streak.
  • Kyrie Irving hasn’t been getting to the basket or the line much, but he’s still been efficient, shooting 70% inside the arc and 39% from beyond. His true shooting percentage of 67.1% ranks fifth among the 40 players who’ve averaged at least 20 points in four games or more.

Coming up: The Mavs still have five games left on a stretch where they’re playing six of seven at home. They’re in San Antonio on Wednesday, but their four home games over the next eight days are against the Kings, Jazz, Nets and Pacers.

Week 3: vs. SAC, @ SAS, vs. UTA*, vs. BKN

#15
Golden State Warriors
Last Week:26↑

Record: 4-3

OffRtg: 117.2 (6) DefRtg: 114.9 (23) NetRtg: +2.4 (13)

Cue the GIF of WWE’s The Undertaker rising up off the mat. After a 0-3 Week 1, the Warriors have won four straight games. The first three came against other teams — the Nuggets, Wolves and Lakers — with winning records, and their win over the Clippers on Sunday came without Stephen Curry and Draymond Green.

Two takeaways

  • The Warriors have seen a pretty significant drop in 3-point rate from when they led the league last season, but they’re still the only team that ranks in the top five in both 3-point percentage (40.0%, second) and the percentage of their shots that have come from beyond the arc (47.2%, fifth). They account for three of the 11 times when a team has made at least 20 3s, with no other team having done it more than once.
  • Curry remains special. He totaled 75 points in the wins over the Wolves and Lakers, getting to the line (where he’s 40-for-40) a ton in Minnesota and then shooting 8-for-14 from 3-point range against L.A. But the Warriors have also scored efficiently (116.2 points per 100 possessions) in his 140 minutes off the floor.

Coming up: The Warriors will have rest advantages for two of the remaining four games on their six-game homestand. But for the second time this season, they’ll be at a disadvantage when they face the Suns (Monday). Curry, Green and Brandin Podziemski all missed the first meeting, when Phoenix scored 131 points on 101 possessions.

Week 3: vs. PHX, vs. MIA, vs. ORL

#16
Toronto Raptors
Last Week:9↓

Record: 3-3
