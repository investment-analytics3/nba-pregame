Team and metric filters and sorting use the row

Each game is graded from the last board sent before tip (the Final when there was one); record, last 3, B2B, power rank (PR) and metrics are as they stood on that board. Rows tagged "rebuilt" come from days rebuilt after the fact using only what was known before tip-off (closing spread, that day's injury report; power rank without ESPN BPI). Favorite row colors: green = top 10, yellow = 11-20, red = 21-30. Opponent row colors are flipped (red = a strong opponent). Covered = the favorite won by more than the spread. Click a team to open that day's board for the game. Click a column title to sort.
