The rule. About 32 hours before a city's daily-high market closes (around 5 PM local the day before), buy NO on every bracket whose best YES bid is 1-4c, 25 contracts at the best price (fewer if the book is thinner). Each win pays 1-4c a contract; each loss costs 96-99c.
Why. Two public studies found weather forecasts don't beat these markets, but long-shot brackets settle YES less often than their price. Our check on Oct 6 - Nov 25 data: 645 bets, 4 losses, +0.88c per contract (90% range +0.37 to +1.39) in the 7 original cities only.
Settlement. Kalshi settles on the NWS climate report, usually the next morning; the board marks each bet from Kalshi's official result.
Decision point. Review after ~300-400 main-rule bets. Forecast highs are Open-Meteo, shown for context only.
